Questions

Frequently asked questions

A few honest answers before you call, if yours isn't here, just ask.

What areas of San Diego do you serve?

We're based in and primarily serve San Diego's College Area and Mid-City neighborhoods (92115), with jobs across the greater San Diego area. Call or text to confirm we cover your address before scheduling.

Do you offer one-time clean-ups or only ongoing contracts?

Both. We handle single yard clean-ups for overgrown or neglected properties with no ongoing commitment, as well as recurring monthly maintenance plans for customers who want consistent upkeep.

How do I get a quote?

Give us a call at (619) 822-7689 and tell us about your yard, size, current condition, and what you're hoping to change. Most jobs start with a quick phone conversation.

Do you install sod, or just maintain existing lawns?

Both, full sod installation is one of our specialties for dramatic lawn transformations, and we also provide ongoing lawn and yard maintenance to keep an existing lawn healthy afterward.

What are your hours?

We're generally available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and Saturday mornings. We're closed Sundays. Call ahead to schedule a specific visit.
